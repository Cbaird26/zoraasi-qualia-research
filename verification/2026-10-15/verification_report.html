Phase 2.3 — reset mathematics

The stationary reset Brier derivation is correct. The stated reset BCE 0.6869 is numerically incorrect; the correct value is 0.6873426691572835, rounding to 0.6873. These are known-parameter expected losses at a fixed 29-transition horizon, not the exact finite-training expected scores and not promised one-seed outcomes.

a = p01 = 0.28; b = p11 = 0.92; λ = b − a = 0.64
π = a / (1 − b + a) = 7/9; v = π(1 − π); H = 29
C(q,p) = −q ln(p) − (1−q) ln(1−p)
C_D = (1−π)C(π,a) + π C(π,b)
D = logit(b) − logit(a); g_H = (1−λ^H) / [H(1−λ)]

Stationary reset Brier(t) = v[1 + λ² − 2λ^(t+1)]
Stationary reset BCE(t)   = C_D − v λ^t D
Mean reset Brier = v(1+λ²) − 2vλ²g_H = 0.23007232801466346
Mean reset BCE   = C_D − vλDg_H      = 0.6873426691572835

At t=1 the reset and stateful predictions coincide. Under stationary initialization, their reset score approaches the deranged score as the initial state loses predictive information. The source starts instead with μ0=0.5, so the stationary covariance and stationary table do not apply unchanged.

For the actual source, μ_t = π + (μ0−π)λ^t, with μ0=0.5.
r_j(t) = P(X_t=1 | X_0=j) = π + (j−π)λ^t
q̄ = (1/H) Σ[t=1..H] μ_t = 0.760749295777340

For either loss L = Brier risk or BCE risk:
Reset(t)    = (1−μ0)L(r_0(t),a) + μ0 L(r_1(t),b)
Deranged(t) = (1−μ_(t−1))L(μ_t,a) + μ_(t−1)L(μ_t,b)

Phase 2.4 — stronger exploratory benchmark

Executed 200 primary seeds (20260921–20261120) and 100 additional disjoint seeds (30260921–30261020), with 2 independent streams per seed and 360 scored transitions per stream. Tested stationary Regime A and alternating A/B blocks of 15, 45 and 90 transitions. Nine ordinary arms share data and learned initialization; two explicitly privileged oracle references bring the total to eleven. Hyperparameters were not tuned in this audit; the separate seed range is an exploratory robustness check, not a prospective preregistration.

The fixed EMA rate is the supplied 0.08, not a rate optimized on a validation set. The comparison therefore supports superiority to that specific simple baseline under these settings, not superiority over the EMA family or modern adaptive estimators. The original Regime B (0.18,0.82) is an alternating process with stationary probability 0.5; “sparse” is a misleading description.

Negative differences favor the full engine. It improves over the specified plain EMA in all three switching settings, but degrades performance in stationary A. Removing the self-model lowers mean Brier and BCE in every setting in both seed ranges; for some close comparisons the uncertainty interval still includes zero. Memory removal increases mean loss in these settings, especially 45-step switching, supporting a conditional predictive contribution from that mechanism. The full combination is not the best tested variant.

The scalar-state derangement also lowers average losses relative to the full engine in every tested setting. This does not establish a benefit from properly aligned scalar states. The control copies only valence, arousal, confidence and continuity from another full-engine replica before any current-step outcome is revealed; fast/slow estimates, snapshots and recent history remain with the target. Donor streams have independent observations but the same regime schedule, so the donor can retain relevant regime information. It is not an unconditional independence null or a shuffle of all engine memory.

How to interpret the uncertainty

Intervals are approximate normal Monte Carlo confidence intervals for a mean over simulated runs: mean ± 1.96×SD/√N. For Phase 2.4, first average the two streams within each seed, then use seeds as the replicate unit. Paired comparisons subtract scores within the same seed; transitions and shared-donor streams are not treated as independent samples. Scenarios share common random numbers and are not independent replications of one another. These intervals are not individual-run prediction intervals, human-population inference, or multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory tests. Raw per-seed and per-stream outputs are supplied for alternative analyses.

Scientific scope and remaining work

The terms valence, arousal, self-model and continuity label numerical controller variables. Episodic memory here means stored transition-parameter snapshots. Neither predictive success nor model agreement establishes feelings, qualia, consciousness, or the MQGT-SCF fields Φc and E. This audit contains synthetic binary streams only, no human subjects, sensor measurements or phenomenological ground truth.

A stronger next forecasting study would select baseline learning rates on independent validation data, add discounted-count/sliding-window and change-detection or latent-regime baselines, score recovery around changes, test randomized or asynchronous regime durations and novel regimes, instrument snapshot retrieval, and include separate valence/continuity ablations. These investigations are not represented as completed by this report. The included current-regime oracle already supplies a known-parameter reference with privileged access to the true regime; the frozen Regime-A oracle makes the uploaded baseline explicit. Neither oracle is an information-matched learned model.
